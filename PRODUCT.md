# PRODUCT.md

Subject: AgriSense AI, a crop research project exploring drought-related soil hardening and root growth with a 1,000-row formula-generated dataset.
Audience: Students, judges and curious growers who need to understand both the analysis and its limits.
Primary job: Follow the question from dataset through findings to validation limits, without mistaking simulation for field evidence.
Voice: Plain and exact, like a field notebook. “The generator encodes this relationship; the dots are not measured plants.”
Not: A live sensor service, a verified agronomy adviser, or evidence that hydrogel improves crops.
Success: A visitor can identify the data source, interpret each chart, and find an official source for a real farm decision.
